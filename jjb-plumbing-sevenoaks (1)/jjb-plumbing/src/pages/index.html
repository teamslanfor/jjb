FAQs

Common questions

Which areas do you cover?{{icon:plus}}

We’re based in Sevenoaks, Kent, and cover the town and the surrounding area. Not sure if we cover you? Call Jorge on {{site.phone}}.

How often should my boiler be serviced?{{icon:plus}}

Once a year. Most manufacturers recommend it, it’s usually needed to keep the warranty valid, and it helps the boiler run safely and efficiently.

Why are some of my radiators cold?{{icon:plus}}

Often it’s trapped air, a stuck valve or sludge in the system. We can find the cause and fix it, from bleeding and balancing to replacing valves or radiators.

Do you take on small jobs?{{icon:plus}}

Yes. Dripping taps, running toilets and small leaks are all welcome.

I have a leak or smell gas. What should I do?{{icon:plus}}

For a leak, turn off the stopcock (usually under the kitchen sink) and call {{site.phone}}. If you smell gas, leave the property and call the National Gas Emergency number on 0800 111 999 straight away.
